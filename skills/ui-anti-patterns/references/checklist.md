# Reviewer checklist

Use it on a rendered screen at desktop width and at 390 pixels. Count the tells. About three is normal. Eight or more reads as generic. Review by group because tells arrive together.

## Colour

- [ ] No default indigo or violet accent.
- [ ] No purple, blue, cyan or pink gradient on backgrounds, buttons or charts.
- [ ] No gradient text.
- [ ] Charts use palette tokens with a stated meaning.
- [ ] Text meets 4.5 to 1 in light and dark themes, also in bright light on a phone.
- [ ] The accent appears about twice per screen at most.

## Type

- [ ] A visible size and weight gap between levels.
- [ ] Display, body and mono roles are defined and used.
- [ ] No single accented word in a heading, no monospace headings.
- [ ] No tracked all-caps labels used as decoration.
- [ ] Nothing below 11 pixels.

## Components

- [ ] No emoji used as icons.
- [ ] No three-up icon cards, no card per KPI.
- [ ] No coloured left borders as decoration.
- [ ] No frosted glass, no heavy shadows on ordinary cards, no neon glow.
- [ ] Not everything is a card. Whitespace and dividers do some of the grouping.
- [ ] At least two radius steps.
- [ ] No eyebrow pill above the headline.
- [ ] Buttons follow one system, have real labels, and show hover, pressed and focus states.
- [ ] Touch targets are at least 44 pixels on phones.
- [ ] Spacing sits on one scale.

## Motion and finish

- [ ] Nothing fades in on scroll. Nothing follows the cursor. Nothing floats.
- [ ] Motion answers a user action, and reduced motion is honoured.
- [ ] No orbs, grain, blobs or waves behind content.
- [ ] No placeholder images.

## Copy

- [ ] No em dashes, middle dots, arrows on links, or semicolons and colons in short copy.
- [ ] No buzzwords. Statements are concrete and from the user's point of view.
- [ ] No invented numbers. Every figure has a source and a time, or is labelled as sample data.
- [ ] No filler text. Empty states name the next action.
- [ ] No claim of coverage or safety without evidence.

## Recording the result

Write the tell count per screen, list the tells found, and list those removed since the last review. A screen with more than seven tells goes back for rework before any polish.
